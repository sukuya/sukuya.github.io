**May 2019:**

I will be heading to Singapore to join the Machine Translation team at Rakuten Institute of Technology, Singapore as a Research Scientist. My work will focus on working with the production team in deploying the Neural Translation models on CPU via TorchScript and ONNX. I am excited to work on a piece of technology that at its core enables people to connect at scale.

I will be working at [Rakuten Institute of Technology](https://rit.rakuten.com/), Singapore with [Li Ling Tan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alvations/), who is the lead researcher at Rakuten Institute of Technology, Singapore and [Ewa Szymanska](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ewa-szymanska-phd-276656a/) who is the research manager.
